# ANTICIPO Lite

The local pilot stores only support flags, opaque subject token, optional group, review date, and revocation time. It excludes diagnoses, medicines, national IDs, insurance data, exact addresses, and family detail. Expired or revoked profiles are never presented as current facts. Profiles are intended for a drill and require local privacy, key-management, operational, municipal, fire-service, and legal approval before any real deployment.
